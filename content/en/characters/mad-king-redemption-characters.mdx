## Where Redemption Lives in a Roguelite Loop

Roguelite structure changes how a redemption story gets told. In a run-based game, you fail, return, and grow stronger through upgrades and unlocked skills. That loop is inherently a story about repeated attempts — which maps neatly onto a king who tried to bring someone back and broke himself doing it.

That parallel is thematically suggestive, not developer-confirmed. The store page does not say the Mad King's magic is a metaphor for the player's own retry loop, and no official material describes a narrative justification for runs. It is fair to read the game that way; it is not fair to present that reading as lore.

What *is* documented is the mechanical shape: you upgrade heroes and unlock multiple skills across attempts. That is the system you will actually interact with, and it is the one worth thinking about early.
