## Where Community Advice Helps and Where It Misleads

Third-party guides are genuinely useful for one thing: describing the feel of a build in a specific patch. They are weak at everything else, because Early Access balance shifts and because guide writers rarely document which build they tested.

Community observations are most reliable when they are structural rather than numerical — for example, "runs reward learning enemy patterns before you invest in upgrades" is a claim you can test yourself in an hour. They are least reliable when they are precise: exact damage multipliers, best-in-slot skill orders, or unlock costs. The store page does not document those, and a guide that presents them without qualification is describing one player's run, not the game's design.

Use community material as a starting hypothesis. Confirm it in your own runs. That is the only method that survives a patch.
