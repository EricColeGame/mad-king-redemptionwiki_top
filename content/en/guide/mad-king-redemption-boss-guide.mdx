## The Roguelite Question: What "Deep" Means in Practice

"Deep roguelite elements" is the phrase most likely to mislead. In the genre, roguelite usually means some combination of run-based progression, randomized upgrades, and persistent unlocks between attempts. But the phrase alone does not tell you the ratio.

The practical question for a beat'em'up is where the roguelite layer sits. Two common models exist. In one, the stage is fixed and the randomization lives in upgrade choices offered between fights. In the other, stage layout, enemy composition, and rewards all shift per run. The store page does not commit to either, and no verified source describes the randomization scope.

Because this is Early Access, the honest answer is that the balance can shift. Roguelite tuning is one of the most commonly adjusted systems in Early Access, precisely because drop rates and upgrade pools are easy to iterate on. If you are deciding whether to buy in now, treat any specific claim about run structure as provisional until you have played a few runs yourself.
