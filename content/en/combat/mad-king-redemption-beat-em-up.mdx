## What Mad King Redemption Is, According to the Official Store Page

The [Mad King Redemption Steam page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2369580/) lists SECRET MISSION as the developer and dates the Early Access release to 14 May 2026. That is the anchor for everything below.

The advertised premise, per the store description: the kingdom has fallen to the Mad King, who turned to forbidden magic in an attempt to resuscitate the queen. The attempt cost him his sanity. You fight your way through hordes of enemies in brawler-style combat against that backdrop.

The description also frames the game as a beat'em'up that combines old-school controls and a retro feel with 3D mechanics, built around deep roguelite elements, hero upgrades, and multiple unlockable skills. Those are the advertised pillars. Nothing on the page commits to a hero count, a skill tree shape, a boss roster, or any damage values.

That restraint matters. A lot of search traffic around this game assumes a wiki-deep system already exists. It does not, at least not from the developer.

## The "Redemption" Framing: Title Versus Documented Story

Here is the honest answer about the "redemption" in the name: the official description explains the King's fall, not his recovery. It says he lost his sanity after the forbidden magic failed to bring the queen back. It does not state that the player redeems him, plays as him, or restores him.

So the title reads as thematic rather than literal. Two readings are consistent with the published text:

- The kingdom is what needs redeeming, and the Mad King is the obstacle.
- The player character is someone else entirely, fighting through a ruined realm.

Which one the game commits to is a story question, and the store page does not settle it. If you want a definitive answer, the in-game codex and the game's own narrative text are the authority, not third-party summaries.
