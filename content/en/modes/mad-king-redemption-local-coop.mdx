## Why "Mad King Redemption" Sets Up a Roguelite Loop

The name is doing thematic work, and the store description supports reading it that way. The Mad King is not a distant villain you hear about in cutscenes; he is the reason the kingdom collapsed, and the reason its streets are now full of enemies. A run-based brawler fits that setup because each attempt is a fresh push into a fallen kingdom, and the roguelite layer is what lets you carry something forward.

The word "redemption" is the interesting part, and it is worth being careful here. The official description does not explain whether redemption refers to the king, to the heroes fighting through his kingdom, or to the kingdom itself. Anything more specific than that would be speculation. If you want a clear answer, watch the in-game narrative content rather than trusting a summary.

What the description does support is the mechanical shape: heroes get upgraded, skills get unlocked, and enemies come in hordes. That is a loop, not a one-time campaign progression, and it is the reason the roguelite tag appears in the official text rather than being a community label.
