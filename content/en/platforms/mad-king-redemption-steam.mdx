## Where Beat'em'Up Meets Roguelite

The tension between these two genres is the most interesting thing about the game's design brief, so it is worth being precise about it.

A traditional beat'em'up gives you a fixed character with a fixed moveset and asks you to master a fixed set of encounters. A roguelite gives you a randomized run and expects you to fail, learn, and return stronger. When you combine them, the combat system has to survive both scrutiny from players who expect arcade precision and players who expect build variety between runs.

The advertised answer here is hero upgrades and skill unlocks. The store page confirms that multiple skills exist and that heroes can be upgraded. It does not publish a hero list, a skill tree, or any per-character statistics. That is normal for an Early Access listing, and it means anyone claiming to have a complete roster or a full upgrade path is working from the game itself, not from developer documentation. If you want authoritative answers on what a specific hero can do, the in-game codex is the place to look, and it may change between patches.
